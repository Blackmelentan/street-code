The finish line

Every service has a finish line. You can see it coming.

Tell Street Code what oil went in and the odometer at the time. It works out when that oil runs out, by distance or by date, whichever comes first, and learns how far you really drive to predict the day.

  • Green to red as the miles build up, with alerts at 80%, 95% and when it is overdue.
  • Different oils, different limits: mineral, semi-synthetic, full synthetic.
  • Cars, motorbikes, taxis, trucks and tractors. Tractors count engine hours, not kilometres.
  • Car wash reminders, insurance, road tax and roadworthiness dates in the same place.

Garages and mechanics

One mechanic. One account. As many garages as they work for.

A mechanic can be an employee at one garage and the owner of another. The boss manages the team: invite by phone number, set roles, suspend or remove someone, all from the app. Nobody can appoint someone above their own rank.

  • Open a job card, quote it, and let the owner approve before anything touches their records.
  • Finish the job and the work is sealed into the vehicle passport under your garage's name.
  • Verified garages appear in the directory. Customers can trust the stamp.

How a passport stays honest

  1. Written by the right person. Only the garage that did the work can seal a service. Not the owner, not us.
  2. Hashed and chained. Each record contains the fingerprint of the one before. Change a word anywhere and the chain breaks.
  3. Signed. Every record carries an Ed25519 signature. Anyone can check it with our public key, without trusting our server.
  4. Odometers only go up. A lower reading is refused, recorded and reported to the owner.

Trust

For the police

Check a plate. Know the rest.

One lookup shows registration, insurance, road tax, roadworthiness, open citations, who is declared as driving and on whose authority, and whether their licence covers the vehicle. Red alerts and persons of interest appear with their instruction.

Open Police Command

Officers are added by an administrator. There is no self-service sign-up.

Power with a paper trail

  • Every lookup is recorded by the server with the reason and the place. It cannot be skipped or edited.
  • Two people for a person flag. The creator can never approve their own.
  • Persons of interest expire after at most 90 days unless renewed.
  • Restricted detail stays restricted. Officers see the summary and instruction; supervisors see the rest.
